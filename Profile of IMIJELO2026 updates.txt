Imijelo Yophuhliso Profile 
                                                                             
 
Imijelo Yophuhliso is a community development organisation (NPO) that advances youth 
empowerment, community wellbeing, disability inclusion, intergenerational learning, and social 
participation through life-skills education, community-based rehabilitation (CBR), community 
engagement, and research. 
 
Guided by the theory of change: 
Identity → Behaviour → Agency → Opportunity 
Imijelo offers community-based platforms that enable people, families, and communities to build 
agency, foster inclusion, and access opportunities that improve wellbeing.  
 
Under this umbrella, the Organisation's work is organised into three strategic pillars. 
 
Pillar 1: Future Readiness and Opportunity Development 
Motto: Personal Growth Through Money Management 
Identity → Behaviour → Agency → Opportunity  
Using money management as a pathway to personal growth, informed choices, and future readiness. 
 
This pillar incorporates the Smart Generation Movement (SGM) as the Organisation's flagship youth 
development platform. 
 
The Smart Generation Movement (SGM) 
Motto: Personal growth through money management 
The programme supports young people to develop a strong sense of identity, make positive life choices, 
build confidence and agency, and pursue opportunities for their future. 
Focus areas include: 
• 
Money management 
• 
Career readiness 
• 
Leadership development 
• 
Mentorship 
• 
Future planning 
• 
Agency  
Through this work, young people are equipped with the knowledge, skills, networks, and confidence 
needed to pursue opportunities, make informed life choices, and successfully navigate their transition 
into adulthood. 
 
Pillar 2: Community Wellbeing and Inclusion 
Motto: Collective Agency for Wellbeing and Inclusion 
This pillar brings together initiatives that strengthen wellbeing, inclusion, family resilience, and 
community participation. 
Activities include: 
• 
Disability inclusion programmes 
• 
Parent support groups for caregivers of children with disabilities 
• 
Youth wellbeing initiatives 
• 
Savings & Investment Groups

The Savings and Investment model addresses socio-economic challenges and youth unemployment. 
Members use pooled savings to strengthen financial resilience and initiate income-generating activities, 
promoting sustainable livelihoods and economic inclusion for youth, including youth with disabilities. 
The inclusion of parent support groups strengthens families, promotes peer learning among caregivers, 
improves support for children with disabilities, and contributes to the development of more inclusive 
communities. 
 
Pillar 3: Intergenerational Care and Learning 
Motto: Learning Across Generations, Growing Together 
This pillar represents a distinctive Imijelo niche, recognising that inclusive communities and active 
citizenship are strengthened when children, youth, and older adults meaningfully engage in 
intergenerational care, learning, and knowledge exchange. 
 
Focus areas include: 
• 
Intergenerational Care and Learning 
• 
Community Participation and Social Inclusion 
• 
Active Citizenship

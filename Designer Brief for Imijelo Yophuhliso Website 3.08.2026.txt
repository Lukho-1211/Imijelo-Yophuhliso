Strategic Positioning 
Draw inspiration from Ashoka's approach to framing social change by presenting people and 
communities as agents of change rather than beneficiaries of services. 
The website should reflect Imijelo's philosophy that sustainable change occurs when people, 
families, and communities build agency, strengthen relationships, foster inclusion, and work 
together to create opportunities that improve wellbeing. 
Position Imijelo Yophuhliso not as a charity delivering services to passive recipients, but as a 
community development organisation that enables young people, families, caregivers, 
children with disabilities, older adults, and communities to build agency, strengthen 
relationships, participate actively in community life, and access opportunities that improve 
wellbeing. 
The tone should be hopeful, empowering, and solutions-oriented. Participants should be 
portrayed as active citizens, leaders, mentors, learners, caregivers, and contributors to 
community wellbeing. 
Content should emphasise community assets, strengths, local knowledge, relationships, 
participation, resilience, and collective action rather than focusing primarily on lack/deficits, 
needs, or vulnerability.

Core Narrative 
The website should communicate that meaningful social change occurs when people, 
families, and communities build agency, strengthen relationships, foster inclusion, and 
participate actively in community life, leading to greater access to opportunities that improve 
wellbeing. 
This narrative should reflect Imijelo's Theory of Change: Identity → Behaviour → Agency 
→ Opportunity
